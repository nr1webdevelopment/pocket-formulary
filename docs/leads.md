# Leads and known gaps (v2.0.0)

Bangkok leads are in [bangkok-leads.md](bangkok-leads.md). This file covers the other nine cities. Everything here is a lead to research or verify, not data.

## All cities

- **Every provider is `unverified`.** The first job is phone or email checks, starting with HIV providers that list PEP.
- **ADHD and diabetes lists are short** (1–3 providers per city outside Bangkok). Most cities have many more hospital endocrine and psychiatry departments; we only added ones whose own pages state the service.
- **Translations** in `i18n/` were machine-produced. Medical wording in Thai, Chinese and Portuguese most needs native review.

## Macau (3 providers)

- Very few public sources list HIV, ADHD or diabetes services by clinic. Most records have services set to `null`.
- Leads: Health Bureau (Serviços de Saúde) health centres, Kiang Wu Hospital departments, Macau AIDS-related NGOs. Confirm by phone.

## Taipei (6 providers)

- Taiwan CDC's lists of designated HIV hospitals and PrEP providers are published as PDFs that we couldn't retrieve (blocked to automated access), and data.gov.tw was also blocked. Someone should download those lists by hand and add the Taipei entries.

## Shanghai (11 providers)

- The Shanghai Municipal Health Commission site was blocked to automated access. Its lists of HIV testing sites and designated treatment hospitals should be checked by hand.
- Foreigners' access to free ART in Mainland China differs from citizens'. Confirm with each hospital.

## Guadalajara (9 providers)

- Leads: more Jalisco state health (SSJ) CAPASITS and IMSS hospital services, private endocrinology and child psychiatry clinics.

## Singapore, Hong Kong, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Lisbon

- HIV coverage is reasonable. ADHD and diabetes coverage is thin. Hong Kong: Hospital Authority psychiatry and diabetes centres. Singapore: polyclinic diabetes services, IMH and KKH child psychiatry. US cities: county clinics and FQHCs. Lisbon: SNS hospital endocrinology and child psychiatry departments.
