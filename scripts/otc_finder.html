Medicines

See a doctor or go to a clinic instead if…

  • Fever above 39.4 °C / 103 °F, or any fever lasting more than 3 days
  • Chest pain, trouble breathing, a stiff neck, confusion, or the worst headache of your life
  • Blood in stool or vomit, or diarrhoea with high fever (especially after street food or travel)
  • Swelling of the face, lips or tongue after a bite, food or medicine
  • You're pregnant or breastfeeding, buying for a child, take regular prescription medicine, or symptoms last more than a week

General information only, not medical advice. Always read the label and ask the pharmacist, especially about dose, other medicines you take, and any health conditions. Brands, strengths and what's sold over the counter change between countries and over time; the brands listed are common examples, not a complete list. When in doubt, show the pharmacist the "Ask for" name.
