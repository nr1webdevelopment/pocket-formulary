- `screenshots/` — one stitched full-page screenshot per page (1280px desktop layout, scaled to 800px wide; site footer cropped)

## Notes

- Several pages are dated: the FAQ says "Last Reviewed: March 25, 2010" and is CDC-sourced; PrEP content refers to Truvada only and cites thebody.com; ELISA/Western blot pages describe older testing algorithms; the risk-of-exposure article is a CATIE (Canada) reprint. Cross-check against current CDC/NIH guidance before using any of it.
- All 17 listed clinical trials were "Not recruiting" at capture time.
- Every condition page repeats the same "Condition Spotlight" video and the two clinics (Infectious Diseases Clinic 650-736-5200; Positive Care Clinic 650-723-9001).
